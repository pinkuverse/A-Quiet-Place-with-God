🔍 Inductive Bible Study

Observe → Question → Apply. Slow down and let the Scripture teach you ♡

1Choose a Scripture
2Observation
Ask: Who? What? Where? When? Which words repeat? What stands out? Write only what the text says. No guessing yet.
3Questions
What does it mean? Why did the writer say it? What is hard to understand? What does it show about God and about people?
4Timeless Discipline
Put the lasting truth in your own words: a short quote or saying that could help anyone, in any time. Then live it out today.
